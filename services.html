Services

Two ways to build your leaders.

Whether you're developing a whole management layer or investing in one key leader, Refynity meets your team where they are — and builds from there.

Service 01

3-Day Training Series

An immersive, cohort-based intensive that takes your management team through the full Refynity method — three days mapped to the three disciplines that give the company its name.

Cohort-based In-person or virtual For management teams

What the three days cover

Day 01 · Reflection

Leaders map their own patterns, triggers, communication style, and blind spots — building the self-awareness to lead from clarity rather than reaction.

Day 02 · Refinement

Practical frameworks for the hard parts of management — accountability, feedback, delegation, and the conversations most leaders avoid.

Day 03 · Infinity

Turning new skills into lasting practice — building a shared team vision and the habits that keep leaders evolving long after the training ends.

Service 02

1:1 Coaching Package

Focused, ongoing coaching for individual leaders — private, practical, and paced to the real challenges as they happen.

One-on-one sessions tailored to the leader in front of us — a newly promoted manager finding their footing, or a senior leader navigating a bigger mandate. We work on the actual situations they're facing, in real time.

  • Regular one-on-one sessions built around real situations
  • A development plan tailored to the individual leader
  • Support between sessions when hard moments come up
  • Progress you can see — in the leader and in their team
